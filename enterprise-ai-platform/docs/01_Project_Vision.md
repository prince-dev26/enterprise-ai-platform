# Enterprise AI Knowledge Platform

## Vision

The Enterprise AI Knowledge Platform is a production-ready AI SaaS application that enables organizations to securely upload, organize, and search their internal documents using Artificial Intelligence.

The platform allows employees to ask natural language questions and receive accurate answers with citations from company documents using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

---

## Problem Statement

Companies store thousands of PDFs, Word documents, policies, manuals, and technical documentation.

Finding the right information is time-consuming.

This platform solves that problem by allowing employees to chat with their organization's knowledge base using AI.
